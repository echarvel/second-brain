- 11:58 #[[relevant-notes]]#minimalism
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[intentionally-promoting-things-value-removal|By intentionally promoting the things we most value by the removal of the things that distracts us from them, we gain time and energy we would otherwise waste on their maintenance; money, since we buy fewer things; freedom, since possessions end up owning us; relaxation, since clutter creates stress; and focus, since there are less things competing for our attention. One should only own the essential things that make life better.]]
    - #literature-notes Minimalism is related to essentialism. In essentialism, we remove the inconsequential and unimportant and focus on what matters more. Minimalism is mostly about removing material possessions. In other words, clutter from our lives. You could say that minimalism is the concrete aspect of essentialism. One cannot be an essentialist without being a minimalist.
