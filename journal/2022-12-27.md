# December 27th, 2022

- #[[relevant-notes]]#[[existential-humanistic-therapy]]
  - #[[permanent-notes]] [[purpose-therapy-help-person-cope-adapt-tool|The purpose of therapy should not only be to help the person cope or adapt. It should be a tool that helps the individual find their true self and their purpose. This can be done by helping the individual move from a mechanized existence, to one where he is free to choose (within human limits), able to reflect on that freedom, and own the responsibility to determine his path in life.]]
    - #fleeting-notes Cognitive Behavioral Therapy can be considered a convenient strategy, which is not so much in line with the true purpose of the individual. This is a wishy-washy concept, that I've always thought cognitive behavioral therapist seem not to care too much about. It has always seemed to me a conveyor belt type approach.  Yes, it fixes the individual, gives them "coping" mechanisms, but does it guide them towards any real change? I'm not so sure.
