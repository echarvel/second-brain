# September 4th, 2022

- 10:59#[[relevant-notes]]#daily-review
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[reviewing-completed-tasks-previous-day-give-sense|Reviewing completed tasks on the previous day will give you a sense of accomplishment and making sure you make progress on the important tasks by reviewing and adjusting your plan for the day is the best way to set you up for success.]]
- 11:00#[[relevant-notes]]#weekly-review
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[end-every-week-score-important-retrospective-week|At the end of every week, we must score it. It is important to do a retrospective on the week, see what went wrong and opportunities to improve. It is also important to track you leading and lagging indicators for the week to see if there is an issue with our planning and execution framework. For this, keep a weekly scorecard that tracks task accomplishment and habit consistency. If you have lagging indicators, track these as well. Add any action items that result from incomplete or previous work to this or other weeks, or add a new goal to be addressed at a later date. Finally, review your vision and aspirational goals to make sure we don't lose track of the purpose of each goal.]]
- 11:04#[[relevant-notes]]#monthly-review
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[monthly-review-higher-level-version-weekly-review|The monthly review is a higher-level version of the weekly review. Because we are creating quarterly plans, and because goals were designed to track towards our vision, monthly reviews are not about changing direction, but about making sure that we're executing according to plan. Again, for this review, we sum up our weekly scorecards for leading and lagging indicators, as well as habit consistency.]]
- 11:06#[[relevant-notes]]#quarterly-review
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[after-quarter-sum-up-went-right-went|This is done after the quarter is done. We sum up what went right and what went wrong. If everything went according to plan, then we completed our twelve-week goals. If we pushed ourselves hard enough, then it is possible that some of the goals were completed part way. We look at our final scores and determine if we got closer to our aspirational goals and our vision and why, perhaps in a quick essay or journal entry. We determine if the aspirational goals that we're trying to achieve are still valid or if we need to prioritize another aspirational goal for the following quarter and create new ones if needed.]]
- 11:08#[[relevant-notes]]#annual-review
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[annual-review-like-reset-button-during-annual|An annual review is like a reset button. During the annual review we review our life categories and adjust them to our current life situation.  For each life category, we review all areas and see if there are areas that are no longer relevant and remove them or adjust them.  We review our values to make sure they still reflect what is important in our lives.  We review and adjust our vision according to our values.  We rewrite the vision synopsis.  We create new aspirational goals if needed and remove the ones that are no longer relevant.]]
