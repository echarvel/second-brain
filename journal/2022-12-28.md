- [[existential-humanistic-therapy]]
  - [[purpose-therapy-help-person-cope-adapt-tool]]
  - Moving from a mechanized, robotic, externalized life to an internal, emotional, intellectual life, causes crisis, or angst. But only through this shift, can we reach a fuller consciousness and potential.
  - This are the core tenets of existentialist-humanism. We are free to become ourselves. We will encounter limitations regarding what we become, it's not "the sky is the limit" for anyone. Once we understand it, then we get to reflect on this: on our limitations and on our "becoming". Finally, understanding who we are determines our actions, and acting according to who we are, is our responsibility.
  - This is one of the main reasons that I dislike the idea behind therapy, church and other "mind bending" "brain washing" approaches to life.
