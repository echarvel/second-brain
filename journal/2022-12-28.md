# December 28th, 2022

- #[[relevant-notes]] #[[existential-humanistic-therapy]]
  - #[[permanent-notes]][[purpose-therapy-help-person-cope-adapt-tool|The purpose of therapy should not only be to help the person cope or adapt. It should be a tool that helps the individual find their true self and their purpose. This can be done by helping the individual move from a mechanized existence, to one where he is free to choose (within human limits), able to reflect on that freedom, and own the responsibility to determine his path in life.]]
  - #fleeting-notesMoving from a mechanized, robotic, externalized life to an internal, emotional, intellectual life, causes crisis, or angst. But only through this shift, can we reach a fuller consciousness and potential.
  - #fleeting-notes This are the core tenets of existentialist-humanism. We are free to become ourselves. We will encounter limitations regarding what we become, it's not "the sky is the limit" for anyone. Once we understand it, then we get to reflect on this: on our limitations and on our "becoming". Finally, understanding who we are determines our actions, and acting according to who we are, is our responsibility.
