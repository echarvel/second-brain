  - #[[permanent-notes]] [[multifaceted-beings-important-identify-facets|We are multifaceted beings, and it is important to identify the facets that form the different threads of our lives. Without segmenting our lives into areas of focus, it becomes difficult to create a well-rounded and balanced life.  Categories are general in nature, and many areas and values sprout from the way we define them. They are also synergistic in the sense that improvement in one category may extend or accelerate improvement in other categories.]]

    - #fleeting-notes Determining our beliefs and our values can sometimes feel like we're pulling them from the ether. They're there, since they make us act in a certain way, but they're there like a blob, not as distinct entities.
      - Because of this, we tend to work harder on the things that we like, not necessarily the things that we value. We also tend to focus on negative or limiting beliefs, instead of values.
      - Identifying top level categories allows us to create a balanced life.
    - #literature-notes Balancing the different Life Categories can be tricky but failing to do so can result in burnout and lack of overall fulfillment. It can feel like one area of your life is draining your energy, stealing your joy and subverting your life purpose and your true self. However, life balance is not about equal time in every category. Life balance is more about intentional imbalance. Life balance is achieved when you spend your time and energy on the things you choose. At different times, you will focus on one area over another, which is fine, if it is on purpose. Life has different seasons, each with its own set of challenges (Brian Moran)
