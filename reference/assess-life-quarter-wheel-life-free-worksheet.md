  - And the simple idea behind the tool is that you can choose which areas of your life you’d like to review and concentrate on and then you assess that area of your life in a scale of 1 to 10. If you choose 1-3 you feel like that area of your life is making you suffer – these areas of your life will need your attention and you need to address the issues that are making you feel this way. 4-7 you feel like you’re hanging in there and surviving – these areas of your life will need your attention and you need to make some changes in your life8-10 you feel like things are going well and you’re thriving – you can celebrate your success, continue doing what you’re doing because it’s working for you and/or make adjustments to thrive even more!
