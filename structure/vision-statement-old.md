- **The wise man built his house upon the rock.** I Identify distractions and eliminate them, remembering that "less is more" and that external order and cleanliness reflect an uncluttered mind. I cultivate philosophical virtue because it provides a solid foundation for life. #Stability
- **Without health, there is nothing**. I Sleep well, get strong, and eat healthy. I fill my happiness jar while minimizing negativity. When needed, I seek medical and mental help. #Self
- **Make boring jobs interesting, and if you can't, leave them behind.** I work in a place where they appreciate my talent, but where I can also a learn from others and hone my skills. If my workplace becomes boring, it is because it's not challenging me in the direction that interests me. #Independence #Carreer
- **I am the master of my fate, I am the captain of my soul.**I strive to free my mind of biases and limiting beliefs and attain the independence that is granted when caring for the things that are important and nothing else. #Independence #Freedom
- **The best mirror is an old friend.** Connecting to others enhances life, so I try to be likeable and approachable. I actively reach out to friends and family. I love my wife with all my strength and she loves me back in the same way. #Connection
- **Life is a great big canvas; throw all the paint you can on it.** In order to achieve my vision, I Improve constantly and with great discipline. I create frequently and without abandon; admire beauty and creators of beauty; watch great movies; read outstanding books; feel things as deeply as I can. I travel. I choose the things I want to learn and trim out the ones that I don't. #Growth
