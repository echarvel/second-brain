# Aspirational Goals

- #stability
  - I have a beautiful, comfortable, clean, and well-maintained home. No part of my home is awkward, dirty, or disorganized. I know where everything is, and I know where things are when I need them. Things are arranged in a convenient way for the primary use of my home. Broken things get fixed promptly to avoid frustration and to keep my mind undisturbed. I'm the enemy of clutter. #home
  - I have enough things to keep me busy. I don't need more books or more stuff for my personal use.  I only need to replace whatever breaks and whenever it is essential. I have arrived at the stage of my life where it is more important to let go of material things than to acquire more. I must make an effort to give or throw away and not to hoard. #Possessions
  - I study and practice philosophy. Whenever something difficult happens in my life, I have a store of knowledge that guides me and puts it in context. It helps me embrace obstacles with courage and inner stillness. I avoid excess, moderate extremes and gain wisdom as a lifelong student. I achieve wisdom by exercising my freedom to pursue my beliefs and personal values. #philosophy
  - I will achieve complete financial independence in five years. If an unexpected health or financial catastrophe does occur, then my plan has alternate paths so that my goal of financial independence is not derailed.  #security
- #self
  - I choose what I eat carefully, following a diet that is sustainable and delicious. What I ingest provides me with energy and the necessary nutrients to keep by body healthy. I don't over-eat and keep my weight between 165 and 170 pounds.  #Health
  - I am strong, fit, lean, flexible, and mobile. I have enough mobility in my hips and hamstrings to sit down on the floor comfortably. I have attained enough shoulder and back flexibility to do an arch position comfortably. I can perform sets of bodyweight pullups and pushups. I can stand up from a sitting position without using my hands.#Fitness
  - My mind is clear and calm. I listen to my emotions, but they do not control me. I choose my goals carefully so that they fit my passion and I can focus on what is important. I have defeated my monkey mind, and am able to let go of worry, guilt, obsessive thoughts, and harmful compulsions. I use nature to refuel my soul and experience the present. I have a trusted therapy that I visit regularly to get help achieve a clear mind. #mental-clarity
- #independence
  - I can solve diverse types of computational programming problems.  The ability to code efficient and timely solutions is the best way that one can show their ability to code. It is also an important skill to have as a programmer, and it translates to other programming and system design skills. I constantly gain new skills that are relevant to the industry. I apply these skills at my place of employment. For example, this can be a new framework, a new system tool, or a new programming language. #Programming
  - #Carreer   When working, there is no place for distractions. I'm a focus machine and a monster of productivity.  I achieve flow state and block out any disturbances, interruptions, or intrusions. The keyboard while coding becomes the only sound I hear. Procrastination is the enemy.
  - #Freedom   I'm free to choose. I'm not constrained by emotional or financial debt. Guilt is not part of my make-up. Whatever wrongs I've done, I've let them go and forgiven myself. Whatever wrongs have been done to me, I have forgiven. I choose how to react to difficult situations and live only according to my own expectations, which are higher than anyone else's.  Money does not control me either, as I spend below my means so that I'm not a slave to superfluous wants. These two things make me emotionally and financially wealthy and free.
- #connection
  - #social-skills   I make a great first impression by keeping a clean, orderly and sharp appearance. I use my social skills to be engaging and charismatic with others and have developed the ability to be a great conversationalist.
  - #Marriage   I have a strong relationship with my wife based on respect, attention, communication, tolerance, and devotion; a life with common goals where we put each other first above everything else. A close and intimate friendship. We have a great love affair, and we make each other happy.
  - #Friends   I share my quality of life with my friends. I let them influence me and I influence them. I am a great host and make an earnest effort to learn what makes them tick and what makes them feel at ease.  I laugh with them, I dine, travel, and experience the world with friends.
  - #Family   I actively look for ways to interact positively with my family. Sharing a meal, watching a ball game, or drinking a good bottle of wine. I have affectionate reunions with them full of laughter and show my support in tough times. I offer my help without looking for reciprocity. For all of them, especially for my son, I am an example of health, rationality, passion, and tolerance.
- #growth
  - #mind-expansion   I learn with discipline, dream and create without abandon. I Consume edifying intellectual content while having consistent and constant creative output that I perfect with every iteration, which I share with people with similar interests.
  - #Intention   I use an established workflow to set and execute goals and reach a balanced life. This process starts with the definition of purpose, values, and vision within the different facets of life. It drills down to the definition of aspirational goals, includes the execution of tactical goals and their related actions and projects. It also includes an annual, quarterly, monthly, and weekly review process to fine tune the definition and execution of said goals.
  - #fun-and-adventure   I keep a stocked kitchen and love to cook from a large repertoire of healthy recipes that people love to eat. I invite friends and family over to try dishes that I perfect over through diligent practice. I keep a journal of what I try and what I can do to improve.
