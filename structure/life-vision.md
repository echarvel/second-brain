- If you build a house on the sand, it will come down tumbling down. If you build it on a rock, it will withstand storms. Having a physical place to land, gives stability. Not having a cluttered home with things that you don't need gives stability. Having a philosophy of life and not being swayed by every idea, gives stability.
  - **I have a clean organized home, and because of that, it is stress free and pragmatic.** The decorations and furniture make it warm and cozy. I like being at my home above all other places. It is were I go to recharge. My home is where I keep my stuff, and there is a clear organizational pattern as to where things are stored. No drawer overflows, and every drawer and cabinet has a distinct purpose.
  - I avoid hoarding as much as possible and only have a minimal number of things. I make my purchases based on what I need and not based on what I want.** I decide what to keep and what to throw away or sell based on what I will need during the year.** I only keep what is useful and throw away or replace anything that is broken. Everything I own is maintained in working condition.
  - **I approach my life with interest in philosophy, exploring and practicing the wisdom of the old teachings**. I believe in freedom and choice, and that one's existence is predicated by our actions, not by our passions or feelings. I believe that we are rational beings that can be content by the pursuit of wisdom, justice, temperance, and courage. I also believe we are capable of ethical and moral behavior without the need of religion or a big brother, and that science and philosophy can take individuals and humanity to the next level.
  - #Security **I am saving for retirement, and I am covered in case of an unexpected loss of income.** If a medical catastrophe occurs, my family will be able to get the best medical care possible. If I lose my job due to disability, I have an insured source of income that will not take me into a financial crisis. I save enough every year so that I will have enough to retire with a good standard of living. I have no debt.
- #Self I recognize a part of me that is mostly up to me. Building on a foundation of stability, I can maximize my health, fitness and mental clarity.
  - #Health I do what is required to be healthy. Attaining and keeping a healthy weight is very important to me, so I weigh myself every day. **I choose what I eat carefully, and what I ingest provides me with energy and the necessary nutrients to keep by body healthy**. I sleep eight hours a day and have stress coping mechanisms. I make sure that I address any medical issues that come up without procrastination, and I have an annual physical in the first quarter of every year.
  - #Fitness I exercise my strength, flexibility, stamina using a daily workout program of thirty minutes. I avoid the same workout every day or every week to avoid overtraining, and I know my limits, which helps me avoid injury. I walk at least thirty minutes a day.
  - **I practice the denial of the ego, awareness of nature and interconnectedness of the universe. I recognize that attachment is a source of suffering and know that science is limited to what humans can perceive**. I use psychology as a tool to understand my emotions, my interactions with others and stabilize my mind. I take time to disconnect from the daily routine to enhance my creativity.
- #Independence I actively protect my independence so that I can live my life under my own terms. This includes securing an enjoyable way to earn a living, working on something interesting with smart people that I like, and having the presence of mind to make my own choices.
  - #Programming have a job that allows me to hone my skills to perfection while acquiring new relevant skills. I am not overwhelmed by the amount of work and spend time reviewing new and upcoming technology, which I will adopt into our stack when it's beneficial to do so. **I practice algorithms every day and stay up to date with system design concepts, which I apply in my job. I'm involved at every level of the stack, from the database, to the front end and I keep my skills relevant in each stack**. I use the agile methodology, incrementally delivering software that solve user problems, and apply their feedback to improve the product.
  - I wake up every day, excited to go to work, and work on a team that supports what I do and listens to my ideas, for a manager that helps me remove the barriers that can sometimes make work difficult. The company solves interesting problems, and the systems that I work on make an enormous difference to all of their users. I have certain freedom over my own work, and I am allowed to make decisions and implement my ideas. **When I'm working, i don't get easily distracted and always work towards a specific goal**.
  - #Freedom I know what is important, and the reason is that I have a life vision with corresponding goals which lets me choose among an infinite number of alternatives. **I try to live a life free of emotional and financial debt and spend below my means so that I'm not a slave to superfluous wants. This produces wealth, in other words, I take advantage of the magic of compound interest to give me enough resources to be free.**
- #Connection I'm consistently working on my social skills so that I can be engaging and articulate with my friends and family. I try to be an interesting person to others, and display interest in what others have to offer. A lonely life is an incomplete life.
  - **Impressions and presentation are important. ** I have excellent hygiene and never appear disheveled. I dress smartly, with clothing that fits me well and I own a few simple and elegant articles of clothing. I am always alert, remaining undistracted, interested in my surroundings, and paying attention to the people that are with me. 
I am an authentic person, and as such, when interacting with others, I make an effort to be sincere, confident and passionate. I act from genuine feelings. People trust me because I never deceive them. I have a confident attitude against all challenges, and I keep things positive no matter the situation. I have healthy emotions, and I wear them on the surface; I express them and never bury them.
**I am engaging and articulate.** This allows me to communicate my ideas with ease, and being a great listener lets me understand and empathize with others. I know how to make people laugh, and using humor makes people feel comfortable around me
  - #Marriage I share my life with my best friend. We share a long history with each other. We grow every day from each other's experiences through deep conversation and the things we do together. We love to have adventures and experience the world by traveling and exploring. We laugh a lot. **It is a life in which we are a refuge for each other, a shelter from any storm that rages outside of our umbrella of intimacy, tolerance and mutual respect.** We have a strong relationship based on respect, attention, communication, tolerance, and devotion; a life with common goals where we put each other first above everything else. A close and intimate friendship.
  - **I meet with friends and regularly**. During that experience, I let them influence me and I influence them. I make them feel comfortable, and I let myself be vulnerable to them. I listen to what they have to say with complete attention. I laugh with them. I dine, travel, and experience the world with friends.
  - When it comes to friends and family, I have a special place for my son and for my parents. **I actively look for ways to interact with them, show genuine interest in their activities without judgement**. I support their decisions and show them that they're loved and appreciated for who they are. For my son in particular, I am an example of health, rationality, and passion.
- #Growth I align all domains and areas of interest in my life to insure an optimal and synergistic growth.
  - **I learn with discipline, dream, and create with abandon**, and I will do so until my last day on earth. I thirst for quality knowledge and shun information that doesn't contribute to my goals. I'm careful with what I let my brain consume and don't use media consumption as a form of procrastination. I'm constantly searching for ideas and use my imagination to produce creative output.
  - #Intention **I research new ways to improve specific areas of my life and accumulate this knowledge for later reference. I use and analyze this knowledge and make it part of my life by properly digesting it and setting action oriented goals that improve different areas of my life. I pursue these goals with intention and productivity.**
  - I spend time planning and working on projects that will create stimulating experiences for me and others. I take at least two extended vacations every year. I go to concerts, visit museums and go to the theatre frequently. **I eat delicious food that I cook myself and drink good wine**. I share my quality of life with others.
