# Vision Synopsis

- ***My world is created twice, first mentally and then physically. The outside world is the manifestation of my will.*** #Stability
  - *The world I want can only be achieved from a stable base.  *
    - I have a clean, organized, and cozy home so that it can use it as a base of operations and recharging. #Cleanliness #Order #Warmth
    - Having too much stuff is distracting, so I limit my possessions to the few essential things that fit into my world vision. #Possessions
    - I use the wisdom of the ancients, stealing some of their values while forging my own, keeping in mind that the actions that push my humanity forward are the only ones that matter. #Philosophy
    - The world is unpredictable, so I will protect myself from catastrophe in any way I can.  #Security
  - Maintaining peak physical and mental condition is essential to creating my best self.
    - I take the necessary measures to sleep well, eat nutritious foods, avoid overeating and keep a healthy weight.
    - I work out to keep adequate levels of strength, flexibility, and cardiovascular efficiency for my age, without overdoing to avoid injuries.
    - Finally, I possess mental clarity by not ignoring my deeper self and by spending time reflecting on my life. This includes doing what is necessary to maintain healthy emotions, even if it means seeking professional help.
  - Being true to myself can only happen if I can live one's life on their own terms. This means having a means to support myself financially and avoiding superfluous needs.
    - I must have a job that allows me to hone my skills to perfection through practice, while acquiring new and relevant knowledge through training.
    - This job should not consume every second of the day, nor exhaust my mental energy, leaving me unable to live a balanced life. It should make me happy by allowing me to work sane hours on a team that builds cool stuff that helps the world.
    - My outstanding and intelligent coworkers, from whom I learn things every day, should appreciate my work. Finally, the job will provide enough income that I will use to support a frugal way of life, in which I avoid materialistic needs and focus on learning, creating, experiencing, and connecting instead of having. If I can achieve this, it means that I'm wealthy and free.
  - A life lived in isolation is incomplete. Connecting with others enhances every experience and feeling.
    - I will make a conscious effort to be alert, presentable and charismatic. I will strive to make a good impression by acting from genuine feelings, being sincere, confident, and passionate.
    - I will work on being a great communicator by being articulate and interested in the conversations of everyone I talk with.
    - I will work on my sense of humor. I will make people laugh and I will laugh with them.
    - I share my life with my best friend, and we grow from each other's experiences, feelings, knowledge, and laughter.
    - I meet with friends and family regularly and show genuine enthusiasm in their interests, worries and activities.
    - I do what it takes to share a special bond with my son.
  - I align all domains and areas of interest in my life to insure optimal and synergistic growth. I learn with discipline. I dream and create with abandon.  Human beings are not perfect, but they can improve throughout their lives. I'm a research machine into how I can improve all the areas of focus. I process this research, digest it, and choose what to make it my own. Based on my findings, I set goals and pursue them productively and with intention.
