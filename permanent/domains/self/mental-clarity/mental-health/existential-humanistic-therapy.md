The purpose of therapy should not only be to help the person cope or adapt. It should be a tool that helps the individual find their true self and their purpose. This can be done by helping the individual move from a mechanized existence, to one where he is free to choose (within human limits), able to reflect on that freedom, and own the responsibility to determine his path in life.
