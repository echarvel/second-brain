By intentionally promoting the things we most value by the removal of the things that distracts us from them, we gain time and energy we would otherwise waste on their maintenance; money, since we buy fewer things; freedom, since possessions end up owning us; relaxation, since clutter creates stress; and focus, since there are less things competing for our attention. One should only own the essential things that make life better.
