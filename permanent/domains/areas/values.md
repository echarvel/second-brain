We act according to certain principles, tenets, beliefs, or essential intents, which include traits that we were born with, and things that we have acquired through learning and experience. From those personal beliefs, the ones that lead us to live "the good life", the ones that are considered righteous or virtuous, the ones that cause deep satisfaction in our lives, those are our values. It is useful to see facets of human nature in large groups and drill down all the way to individual values. For example, within the domain of independence, we could have the area of Financial independence, and within that area, we could have the value of frugality.
