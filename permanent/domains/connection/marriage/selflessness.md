Marriage is not about sacrifice. I don't believe in martyrdom. Rather, it's about giving yourself over to another person knowing that they will give themselves back in return. Be patient, let things happen when they need to happen. Not everything can be rushed. Be tolerant, identify details that have no importance. Accept aspects that cannot change without recrimination. Be humble. Recognize that I will fall short, will always need to grow, while being compassionate and forgiving.  Don't hold on to grudges.
