# This is done after the quarter is done. We sum up what went right and what went wrong. If everything went according to plan, then we completed our twelve-week goals. If we pushed ourselves hard enough, then it is possible that some of the goals were completed part way. We look at our final scores and determine if we got closer to our aspirational goals and our vision and why, perhaps in a quick essay or journal entry. We determine if the aspirational goals that we're trying to achieve are still valid or if we need to prioritize another aspirational goal for the following quarter and create new ones if needed.
