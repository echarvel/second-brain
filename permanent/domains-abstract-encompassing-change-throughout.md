# Domains are abstract and all encompassing. They may change throughout life, but will only change if your philosophy of life changes. For example, the **Self** domain will encompass personal development in one shape or another, but perhaps it will be in the shape of philosophy one year, and psychology the next. It may also put a greater emphasis on health and fitness, but that emphasis will wane or change drastically as we get older. The **Connection** domain will involve a lot of activity when parenting, but less when the child has grown up. However, the **Self** and **connection** domains will remain valid as a higher level category. Areas are sub level of domain that encompass things that are relevant now, but which might change more frequently
