# One must always be aware of the status of one's health. This includes monitoring things like weight, blood pressure, sleep, calories, and other metrics. It also includes being aware of stress and having avenues to relieve such stress. Also important to listen to your body and avoid over-exercising, out, over-eating, and over-working.
