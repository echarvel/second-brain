# Love is the strongest emotion. The prime motivator. Without love an extraordinary life is not possible.  Love is more about giving than receiving, but it is not about martyrdom. There is no such thing as unconditional love.  Love is about trusting, supporting, and accepting your partner.  It's about mutual growth. Love is about intimacy, the ability to give yourself over completely to someone else and having that person do the same. Know each other in the deepest way possible. Yearn to know what goes through her mind and her heart and let that be the most important thing.
