Every week set up some time for the different types of tasks that you will perform. Block time in your calendar according to context: **Strategic Blocks**, three hour blocks or uninterrupted focus time; **Buffer Blocks**, perform busy work such as phone calls and follow ups; **Breakout blocks**, time to recharge and do things other than working towards goals (leisure time).
