# We like to buy and own things because it gives us a sense of false worth. They make us feel accepted by others, they give us a temporary and false sense of happiness. They make us feel successful. Find that happiness by being generous. Find that acceptance by forging real relationships with friends and family. Find that feeling of success by redefining what success means to you.
