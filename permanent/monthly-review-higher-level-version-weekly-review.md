# The monthly review is a higher-level version of the weekly review. Because we are creating quarterly plans, and because goals were designed to track towards our vision, monthly reviews are not about changing direction, but about making sure that we're executing according to plan. Again, for this review, we sum up our weekly scorecards for leading and lagging indicators, as well as habit consistency.
