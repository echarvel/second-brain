# To start with, select five or six aspirational goals to work on during the year. Come up with specific activities to be performed during that time. They may be ongoing activities (habits), such as "workout every day", or they could be year long projects. They don't have to take a whole year to complete, but this helps define the scope of what you will accomplish throughout the year.  If it's a habit, then they be stated with the help of the PACT acronym (Purposeful, Actionable, Continuous, and Trackable).
