# One's surroundings are the ultimate reflection of one's values. Cleanliness reflects one's thought processes and living in unclean dirty conditions transmits lethargy, dispassion, and passivity. A clean home leaves less places for allergens, germs, and bacteria to hide, thus a clean home is a healthy home. It is easier to rest in a place that smells clean and where one does not breathe in dust with every breath.
