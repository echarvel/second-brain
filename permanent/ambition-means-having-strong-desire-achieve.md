# Ambition means having a strong desire to achieve something. It means owning a piece of the work, the team and the company and acting as such. It means having initiative by keeping my eyes out for what is needed and implementing it without someone asking me to do it. It means pushing my agenda, which is always for personal, team and company benefit.
