There are several things we can measure to make sure we are going to achieve our goals. If you follow SMARTER or PACT guidelines, then your goals will have indicators to measure. At a tactical level, meaning quarterly goals divided into weekly tasks, we should be measuring the percentage of tasks completed during the week, and the percentage of days that we are following through on our habits.  This can be accomplished by software habit tracking tools and the task management tool we choose. These would be our leading indicators. As for lagging indicators, there are some that are easily measured, even weekly, such as weight loss, but others are not, such us focus or relationship goals.
