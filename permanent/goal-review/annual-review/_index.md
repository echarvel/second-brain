An annual review is like a reset button. During the annual review we review our life categories and adjust them to our current life situation.  For each life category, we review all areas and see if there are areas that are no longer relevant and remove them or adjust them.  We review our values to make sure they still reflect what is important in our lives.  We review and adjust our vision according to our values.  We rewrite the vision synopsis.  We create new aspirational goals if needed and remove the ones that are no longer relevant.
