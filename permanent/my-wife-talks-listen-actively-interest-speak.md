# When my wife talks, I listen actively and with interest. I speak the truth while being careful not to hurt her. I'm on the lookout for emotional cues, and know when she's happy or she's sad, and what she likes and doesn't like. She trusts me because my actions reflect our communication. I don't let things fester. I talk about them. It's is also necessary to be empathetic, tender and respectful.
