# To accomplish goals, we must first believe that completing them successfully is possible. To do this we must have an optimistic and positive outlook and have an abundance mindset. When creating a life vision, it is important to keep in mind our habits of thinking. A productive vision can only be attained in a mindset of happiness, personal satisfaction, success and a bright outlook of the future.
