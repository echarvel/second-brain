Self is the domain that contains areas of responsibility and influence that rely or belong mostly to the individual. They have little to do with their financial or social situation, even if they all categories are **interrelated* One can excel in the areas of the self, independently of what else is going on in their lives. In an ideal situation, I am in as much control of my health and fitness as I can, I have a clear mind and I have the drive and will power to steer my life to where I want it to go.
