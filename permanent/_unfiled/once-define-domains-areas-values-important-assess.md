Once you define domains, areas, and values, it is important to assess how well balanced your life is according to these constructs. For this, you can use a radar chart. Arrange all Values in order of domain and area. Reading your definitions for each value, score it from 1 to 10, 10 being what you would consider the perfect score (you're living your belief) and 1 being that you're living the opposite of your belief. You can visualize these scores using a radar chart.
