At the end of every week, we must score it. It is important to do a retrospective on the week, see what went wrong and opportunities to improve. It is also important to track you leading and lagging indicators for the week to see if there is an issue with our planning and execution framework. For this, keep a weekly scorecard that tracks task accomplishment and habit consistency. If you have lagging indicators, track these as well. Add any action items that result from incomplete or previous work to this or other weeks, or add a new goal to be addressed at a later date. Finally, review your vision and aspirational goals to make sure we don't lose track of the purpose of each goal.
