# A life lived in isolation is incomplete, but achieving connection takes skills. To connect effectively, one needs confidence, attractiveness, humor, etc. Social skills don't appear from the void. They must be attended to and developed. One must work at being authentic, attractive, and engaging to lubricate social interactions and interact with friends and family.
