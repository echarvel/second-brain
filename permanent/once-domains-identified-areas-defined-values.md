# Once your Domains are identified, your Areas defined and your [[Values]] selected, then you can start creating goals. Just like your life vision describes your ideal life, aspirational goals describe actions that you need to take in order to bridge the gap between where you are now and what your vision describes. These are long term goals that may last from one to three years.
