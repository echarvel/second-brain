# A home is needed as a base of stability and enjoyment. One needs a place to recharge batteries. A place that is clean, orderly, and warm. An uncluttered place. A home where every room has a function, and the space is respected. A place where everything is "engineered" to be useful, while containing things that reflect our deepest selves. When one goes on vacation, home is the place we feel an overwhelming desire to come back to.
