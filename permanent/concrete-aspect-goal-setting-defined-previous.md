# This is where the more concrete aspect of goal setting is defined. All of the previous steps have been strategic in nature, but a year is long enough that we can lose track of what we're trying to accomplish. The length of a yearly goal is too nebulous for it to make sense at a practical level. This is why we create goals that are just twelve weeks long.  This is the level of planning that we're actually going to work at.
