# Being fllexible and mobile reduces the chance of injury and provides a safety cushion for one's joints.  Teaching your body how to bear weight without compromising your shoulders, knees, back and ankles will avoid overcompensating other joints, which gives mobility a synergistic benefit with other types of training.
