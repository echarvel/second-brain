# Aspirational goals should be HARD. That is, they should be HEARTFELT, requiring emotion, motivation, and ownership, ANIMATED, that is, able to be vividly pictured, REQUIRED, they should have a sense of urgency, and DIFFICULT, to keep us interested and to make us grow as individuals.
